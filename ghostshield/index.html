The Problem: Blurs your own vision too!

  • Unreadable messages: Chats are so heavily blurred that you cannot even read what clients or teammates sent you.
  • Dangerous hover leaks: The moment your cursor moves over a message, the blur removes itself and exposes your chat directly to the stream!
  • Shallow CSS trick: Only injects in-page CSS filters; it has zero control over what GPU screen capture actually transmits.
✔ GhostShield Engineering Fix

The Solution: Crisp to you, solid black to the stream!

  • 100% Crisp Visibility: Read, write, and reply to chats in full crystal clarity with zero eye fatigue or distortion.
  • Native GPU Exclusion: Powered by Windows SetWindowDisplayAffinity(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE) — Zoom, Meet & Teams capture 100% solid black.
  • Automatic Dynamic Switching: No manual windows needed. Switch to your chat tab and it shields instantly; switch back to work tabs and sharing resumes seamlessly.
